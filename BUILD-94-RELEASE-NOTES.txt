ReadPlanner Stable Build 94 — Multi-EPUB Stability
==================================================

Scope
-----
Build 94 is a targeted stability release based on the reported Build 91–93
issues and six exact EPUB files supplied for validation. Stable planned-reading,
highlighting, notes, bookmarks, speech, Journey, Tree, Growth Companion,
Dashboard and check-in behaviour were not redesigned.

Root-cause corrections
----------------------
1. Large, unplanned EPUBs now use a virtual continuous-reading stream. Nearby
   sections are rendered while distant sections retain measured placeholders,
   preventing the whole publication from blocking the browser main thread.
2. EPUB images are decoded only as they approach the viewport instead of all at
   once. Source EPUB Blob persistence is queued outside the critical import path.
3. Virtual sections no longer use layout containment while rendered. The old
   containment could report zero-height sections, overlap chapters, corrupt the
   scroll range and restore a seemingly random location.
4. Free reading remains one uninterrupted book. It contains no daily completion,
   check-in or hidden reading-plan boundary.
5. Exact TOC, search and publisher-link destinations survive Reader rerenders.
   Duplicate short labels are disambiguated with their source word positions, so
   labels such as “Business” do not jump to a different occurrence.
6. Nested publisher TOC parents are preserved. The Reader TOC is independently
   scrollable and no longer closes because a vertical pan is interpreted as a tap.
7. The Sea Reader TOC suppresses leading decorative/front-matter rows and begins
   at Chapter I. Back matter remains available after the chapters.
8. Free Page-by-page mode paginates one internal section at a time and crosses
   section boundaries invisibly. This avoids constructing one enormous CSS
   multicolumn document for a magazine or long novel.
9. The whole-book position rail remains visible, follows real continuous or paged
   progress, and accepts position jumps.
10. Duplicate-import and unfinished-import Confirm/Cancel paths are deterministic.
11. AA sliders use direct pointer coordinates and the font-size range is 13–48 px.

Exact EPUB validation set
-------------------------
- The Sea — John Banville
- Dracula — Bram Stoker
- The Atlantic, 2 February 2026
- The New Yorker, 29 June 2026
- Nature, 1 August 2026
- The Economist, 1 August 2026

Runtime validation covered import, duplicate handling, publisher TOC hierarchy,
TOC scrolling, exact chapter/article destinations, continuous free reading,
position restoration, large-file rendering, whole-book progress, Page-by-page
swiping and section-boundary turns, AA controls, and planned-reading regression.
See MULTI-EPUB-TEST-RESULTS.json and STABILITY-AUDIT.md for details.

Deployment
----------
Upload every file in this folder together. After GitHub Pages finishes deploying,
fully close and reopen the installed ReadPlanner web app. Re-import an EPUB when
you need an already-stored book to receive the new parser output; accepting the
existing-book confirmation refreshes its parsed representation while preserving
its reading records.

Validation boundary
-------------------
The automated runtime tests used mobile Chromium emulation. Real iPhone Safari,
its installed-PWA lifecycle, memory pressure and touch behaviour still require the
owner’s final device test with the supplied EPUBs.
