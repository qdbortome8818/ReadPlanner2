ReadPlanner Stable Build 95 — Reader Precision Polish
=====================================================

Scope
-----
Build 95 is a narrowly targeted correction pass based on the nine iPhone
screenshots reported after Build 94. It changes only the seven requested reader
areas. Planned-reading, highlights, notes, bookmarks, speech, Journey, Tree,
Growth Companion, Dashboard, check-ins, import persistence and library storage
were not redesigned.

Corrections
-----------
1. EPUB bullets now remain immediately beside the first line of their associated
   title or paragraph. Build 94's continuous-reading section wrappers had caused
   the old direct-child CSS selectors to stop matching; Build 95 applies the same
   grid/baseline rule inside every rendered section.
2. The whole-book progress rail starts hidden, appears during active scrolling or
   dragging, follows the current position and fades out after scrolling stops.
3. Reader Contents destinations are now one atomic navigation transaction. The
   selected publisher title, day and paragraph are restored together, eliminating
   the former top-of-section/second-scroll race.
4. The pages-left chip now uses the live word-weighted paragraph position across
   the whole book. Its page count and percentage update as reading advances.
5. Broken internal links in publisher contents pages retain their publisher
   typography as non-clickable styled text. This fixes isolated entries such as
   The New Yorker's “Cartoons” becoming plain black while adjacent entries remain
   red, bold and underlined.
6. The unplanned-book overview and its buttons are fully localized, including
   Book overview, Ready when you are, the explanatory copy, Total words, Whole
   book, Read/Continue without a plan and Share book. The dynamic progress chip is
   also localized after every update.
7. Page-by-page mode has been removed from Reading Settings. Existing saved paged
   preferences migrate once to continuous scrolling, and the paged reader cannot
   activate.

Validation boundary
-------------------
Automated checks used mobile Chromium emulation at 390×844. Final iPhone Safari
and installed-PWA touch behaviour must still be confirmed by the owner with the
same EPUBs. Books imported by an older parser should be re-imported once and the
existing-book refresh confirmed so they receive Build 95 paragraph metadata.
